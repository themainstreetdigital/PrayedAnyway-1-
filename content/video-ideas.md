## Launch sequence recommendation

Open the channel with episodes **1 → 13 → 21 → 3** (silence → Jonah → hesed → Peter).
That sequence shows all the channel's registers in the first month: midnight essay, dark comedy,
word study, redemption arc — and every one of them clips well for Shorts.
