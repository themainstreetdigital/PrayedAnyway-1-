# 01 · Brand Identity & Aesthetic Guide

## Channel essence

**Prayed Anyway** is for people who aren't sure God is listening but haven't stopped talking to Him.

One-line positioning: *Honest faith content for people who feel everything and pretend they don't.*

The four aesthetic pillars and what each one means in practice:

| Pillar | What it looks like | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark** | Low-key lighting, candlelight, night scenes, deep shadows, negative space | Quiet delivery, room tone, silence used on purpose |
| **Moody** | Film grain, fog, rain on glass, desaturated color with one warm accent | Minor-key ambient, tape hiss, lo-fi texture |
| **Hopeful** | A single light source that grows through the video — dawn, ember, lantern | Resolution chords arriving late, warmth entering the VO |
| **Whimsy** | Hand-drawn overlays, paper-cutout animation, floating handwritten text, small impossible details (a fish swallowing a moon) | Playful asides, gentle humor, unexpected metaphors |

The rule that holds them together: **every video starts darker than it ends.**
Darkness is the setting, never the conclusion. Whimsy is the crack the light gets in through.

## Voice & tone (the writing rules)

1. **Talk like a friend at 1am, not a pastor at 11am.** Second person, contractions, short sentences.
2. **Name the doubt before the hope.** Never skip to the resolution. Gen Z smells a sales pitch instantly.
3. **No Christianese without translation.** If we say "sanctification," we immediately say what it actually feels like.
4. **Humor is allowed in the wilderness.** Jonah is funny. Balaam's donkey is funny. Use it — then land the weight.
5. **Never shame the viewer.** No "you should." Prefer "what if" and "maybe the point is."
6. **End open-handed.** A question, an invitation, a small practice — not a command.

Words we use: *honestly, maybe, still, anyway, even here, you're not the first.*
Words we avoid: *blessed hustle, lukewarm, backsliding, "the world," anything that sounds like a tract.*

## Visual identity

### Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Ink Night | `#0E1116` | Backgrounds, letterboxing |
| Shadow | Storm Slate | `#2A3140` | Secondary surfaces, fog tones |
| Mood | Dried Lavender | `#6E5E8C` | Whimsy accents, dream sequences |
| Hope | Candle Amber | `#E0A458` | The light source, key text, end-cards |
| Breath | Bone Paper | `#E8E1D5` | Body text, scripture overlays |
| Rare accent | Ember Red | `#9A3B3B` | Sparingly — lament, blood, urgency |

Grading recipe: crush blacks slightly, desaturate 15–25%, add 35mm grain at low opacity,
subtle vignette, and let **amber be the only thing that ever glows**.

### Typography

- **Scripture / titles:** a humanist serif (EB Garamond or Cormorant) — old text, treated with reverence.
- **Narration captions / whimsy notes:** a handwritten font (Caveat) or actual scanned handwriting — the viewer's margin notes.
- **UI / lower thirds:** a quiet grotesque (Inter) at low contrast.
- Scripture always cited on screen in small caps: `JOHN 1:5`.

### Motion & footage language

- Slow push-ins (2–4% scale over 6–8s). Almost nothing moves fast.
- Cut on the VO's breath, not on the beat of the music.
- Recurring motifs to seed across every video (these become the channel's iconography):
  candle/ember, rain on a window, an empty chair, hands (writing, breaking bread, open palms),
  birds at dawn, a door left ajar.
- Whimsy layer: hand-drawn white/amber line animations over live footage —
  a halo sketched over a stranger, waves drawn around a city bus, arrows, underlines, doodled stars.
- Faceless rules: hands, silhouettes, backs of heads, and feet are allowed; faces never linger in focus.

### Thumbnails

- Ink Night background, one amber subject, 3–6 word handwritten-style title.
- Title states the *tension*, not the topic: not "The Story of Jonah" but **"God sent the storm."**
- One consistent corner mark (small candle icon) so the channel reads as a collection.

## Audio identity

- **VO:** intimate, close-mic, slightly slow, low energy but not flat. Think audiobook-meets-voice-memo.
- **Music:** ambient/neoclassical/lo-fi hybrids — felt piano, tape loops, soft synth pads, distant choir.
  Reference artists for mood: Ólafur Arnalds, Sleeping At Last, Hammock, Forrest Frank's quieter cuts.
- **Sound design:** rain, vinyl crackle, page turns, a struck match (use the match at every "turn toward hope" beat — it becomes a signature).
- **Silence:** at least one intentional 3–5 second hold per video, usually right before the turn.

## The signature structure cue

Every video contains one **"strike"** moment — the match-strike sound + the first amber light + the music's first major chord — placed at the turn from lament to hope (usually 55–70% through). Viewers should learn to feel it coming. That's the brand.
