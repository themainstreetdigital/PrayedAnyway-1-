# 04 · Production Workflow (Faceless)

The full pipeline for one 8–15 min video. Solo, realistic time: **8–14 hours** per episode
once you've done three. Steps are ordered; tools listed as **budget pick / upgrade pick**.

---

## Step 1 — Write (3–4 hrs)

1. Pick the idea + format. Copy `docs/05-script-template.md` into `scripts/`.
2. Write the **cold open and the strike line first**. If those two don't work, the video doesn't.
3. Draft to the beat sheet. Target words: minutes × 130, minus ~15% for breathing room.
4. Pass the script against the voice rules (`01-brand-identity.md`): read it out loud once;
   anything you wouldn't say to a friend at 1am gets rewritten.
5. Mark the script with visual cues in brackets as you go: `[rain on glass]`, `[whimsy: halo drawn over commuter]`, `[STRIKE]`.

Accuracy guardrail: for every scriptural claim, check the passage in two translations
(ESV + NLT is a good pair) and note the reference in the script margin. When scholars disagree, say so on camera — uncertainty honestly held is on-brand.

## Step 2 — Voiceover (0.5–1 hr)

- **Your own voice (recommended):** faceless ≠ voiceless, and a real human voice is a moat.
  Budget: any quiet closet + a USB mic (Samson Q2U / AT2020). Record close, slow, second take is usually the keeper.
- **AI voice (if you won't record):** ElevenLabs — pick one voice and never change it; the voice is the face of the channel. Disclose AI narration in the description.
- Deliver at -16 LUFS-ish for YouTube; leave 2s of room tone at the start for noise profiling.

## Step 3 — Visual gathering (2–3 hrs)

Build a shot list from your bracketed cues, then pull from these layers:

| Layer | Source (budget / upgrade) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cinematic b-roll | Pexels, Pixabay / Storyblocks, Artgrid | Search moody terms: "rain window night", "fog forest", "candle macro", "silhouette walking" |
| AI stills for impossible shots | Free tiers of image tools / Midjourney | Keep one style prompt saved; add grain in edit so AI and stock match |
| Whimsy layer | Draw in Procreate or directly in editor | White/amber line doodles, animated with simple write-on |
| Scripture text | Your own typography (EB Garamond) | Never screenshot a Bible app |
| Texture | Film grain, dust, vinyl overlays (free packs) | The unifying skin over everything |

Faceless rules: silhouettes, hands, backs, feet — never a lingering in-focus face.
Reuse your motif shots (candle, rain, door ajar) across episodes on purpose; repetition builds the brand.

## Step 4 — Music & sound (0.5 hr)

- **Music:** Epidemic Sound or Artlist subscription (budget: YouTube Audio Library + free Pixabay ambient).
  Build a saved palette of ~15 tracks so the channel sounds consistent; search "neoclassical", "ambient piano", "tape".
- **The strike kit:** one match-strike SFX + one warm chord stinger, saved as a project preset, used every episode.
- Mix: VO −16 LUFS, music ducked −22 to −28 under speech, rising in the silence holds.

## Step 5 — Edit (3–4 hrs)

- **Editor:** DaVinci Resolve (free, full color tools — best fit for this aesthetic) / Premiere Pro.
- Order of operations:
  1. Lay VO, cut breaths, lock pacing **before** any visuals.
  2. Rough in b-roll to the script cues. Slow push-ins on everything (keyframe 100→104% scale).
  3. Whimsy pass: hand-drawn overlays at the marked beats.
  4. Color: apply the channel grade (crushed blacks, −20% saturation, amber preserved, grain, vignette). Save it as a preset/LUT after episode 1.
  5. Captions: auto-generate, then correct by hand; style once, save preset.
  6. The silence pass: confirm the 3–5s hold before the strike actually breathes.
- Export: 4K (even from 1080 sources — YouTube gives 4K uploads a better codec), H.264/H.265.

## Step 6 — Package (1 hr)

- **Thumbnail:** Photopea (free) / Photoshop. Ink Night bg, one amber subject, 3–6 handwritten words stating the tension. Make 2 versions; use YouTube's Test & Compare.
- **Title:** felt-need first, ≤60 chars. Pattern: *"[the tension]. [the ancient answer]"* — e.g. "God went silent for 400 years. Here's what that means at 1am."
- **Description:** 2 honest sentences, the scripture references, chapter timestamps, the comment question repeated.
- **Cut the 3 Shorts** (see `03-video-formats.md` clip strategy) while the timeline is still open.

## Step 7 — Publish & tend (ongoing)

- Schedule for evening in your audience's timezone (this content gets watched at night).
- Pin a comment that continues the video's open question. Reply for the first hour.
- Log retention at 24h: note where the graph dips, adjust the next script's pacing there.

---

## Minimum viable kit (total ≈ $0–80 to start)

| Need | $0 path |
|---|---|
| Write | This repo's templates |
| Voice | Phone in a closet, or USB mic (~$60–80) |
| Footage | Pexels + Pixabay |
| Edit + color | DaVinci Resolve |
| Thumbnails | Photopea |
| Music | YouTube Audio Library |

Upgrade in this order once revenue/commitment justifies it: music subscription → mic → stock footage subscription. Never upgrade past what consistency requires — **the schedule is the channel.**
